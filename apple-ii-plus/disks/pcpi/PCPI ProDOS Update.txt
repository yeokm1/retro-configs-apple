Boot Applicard, StarCard or Freed Z80 card under ProDOS.  Support startup w/o floppy disk, transfer files between CP/M and ProDOS.

Updated:

3/10/2009

Include some drivers so folks will have them in one spot.  New files are:

DISK35.DVR  -  Support Apple 3.5" drives. Now works on SuperDrive (800K
              mode only! - May do horrid things on a HD disk)

RAMWK0.DVR
RAMWK1.DVR  -  Applied Engineering RamWorks ramdisk driver.  If you are
              running under ProDOS, you MUST use the 'ramwk1.dvr' to
              reserve aux memory page 1 for ProDOS itself.

FAST2E80.DVR - Fixes problem with inverse alpha characters on enhanced
              IIe.  If you are seeing MouseText characters instead of
              letters, you need this.

RDSK512.DVR -  Configure on board memory on 512k Freed Z80 card as a 390k
              ramdisk.  Do NOT use on 128k cards!
